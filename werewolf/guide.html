Werewolf

Moderating
← Back to the game

You run the game; you don't play it. Everyone else has a secret role on their phone — your job is to call the night out loud, keep track of what happens, and announce it in the morning.

Before you start

  • Deal the roles, then tell everyone to tap to reveal and put their phone face down.
  • Keep your own screen private — your roster is the only place the roles exist.
  • Sit where you can see everyone. You'll need to watch people point with their eyes closed.

Each night

Read the bold lines aloud. The Doctor only appears in games of 6 players or more — skip that step if there isn't one.

  1. "Night falls. Everyone close your eyes."
  2. "Werewolves, open your eyes and look at each other. Agree on one person, and point at them." Give them a moment. Remember who they picked.
  3. "Werewolves, close your eyes."
  4. "Seer, open your eyes. Point at someone." Check your roster. Thumbs up if that person is a werewolf, thumbs down if they aren't.
  5. "Seer, close your eyes."
  6. "Doctor, open your eyes. Point at someone to protect." If they pick the werewolves' target, nobody dies tonight. They may pick themselves.
  7. "Doctor, close your eyes."
  8. "Everyone, open your eyes."

Each day

  1. "[Name] didn't survive the night. They were a [role]." If the Doctor saved them, say "Someone was attacked last night — but they survived" and name nobody.
  2. "Talk. You've got a few minutes." Stay out of it. Don't hint, don't react — you know everything and they'll read your face.
  3. "Time to vote. Point at who you want out, on three. Three, two, one." Most fingers wins. A tie means nobody goes out today.
  4. "[Name] is out. They were a [role]." Then check whether anyone has won. If not, call the night again.

How it ends

  • The village wins the moment the last werewolf is out.
  • The werewolves win as soon as they equal or outnumber everyone else — at that point they can't be outvoted.

Check after every elimination. Games usually end sooner than people expect.

Worth knowing

  • Eliminated players stay at the table but say nothing. No hints, no faces.
  • If someone peeks or opens their eyes early, don't restart — just carry on.
  • Keep nights brisk. Long silences are where the game loses its nerve.
  • Finished a round? Deal again reshuffles the same group. Back to lobby reopens the room so latecomers can join.
