## Standard (~90 words) — recommended

Priscilla Liu is a product and experience leader with 15+ years across energy, proptech
and financial services. She is Director, Sustainability at SP Group, where she leads the
group's sustainability narrative, disclosure data and reporting platform — after building
its product, research and agile practice. Earlier she grew SP's consumer app from 80K to
800K monthly users and led product and UX at PropertyGuru across four Southeast Asian
markets. She has keynoted CHIUXID, facilitated for UXSEA and spoken at CDAO Singapore.

genuinely useful end of AI in research and design.
