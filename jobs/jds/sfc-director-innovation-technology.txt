Singapore Fashion Council — Director, Innovation & Technology

Who We Are

Singapore Fashion Council (SFC), formerly the Textile and Fashion Federation (Singapore), is the official association for the textile and fashion industry in Singapore. SFC aspires to become a Vibrant Asian Hub for Responsible Fashion, focusing on four pillars: Sustainability, Innovation & Technology, Internationalisation, and Retail. SFC works with partners across the value chain to offer thought leadership, networks and resources.

The Role

Reporting to the CEO, you will:

Define and lead the Innovation & Technology pillar strategy in alignment with SFC's broader organisational priorities and ambition to become a leading council in the region for responsible fashion innovation.
Own the development, delivery, and continuous improvement of flagship programmes including TBFI, launchpad and scale-up initiatives, and open innovation challenges.
Drive ecosystem building by cultivating relationships with startups, brands, corporates, investors, accelerators, academic institutions, and public sector partners.
Translate strategic objectives into measurable KPIs and deliverables, including programme outcomes, ecosystem impact, and reporting requirements linked to Enterprise Singapore support.
Lead venture-building and innovation initiatives that strengthen market readiness, investor readiness, and commercialisation pathways for participating companies.
Collaborate closely across SFC's pillars, especially Sustainability and Industry Development, to amplify programme impact and create joined-up value for the industry.
Provide leadership, coaching, and direction to managers and programme staff, building a high-performing team with strong execution discipline and external credibility.
Represent SFC externally as a thought leader and connector, convening stakeholders, identifying opportunities, and helping spark innovation across the fashion and apparel ecosystem.
Manage budgets and programme performance with P&L responsibilities, with potential expansion into fundraising and partnership development.
Support exploratory innovation partnerships and special projects that align with SFC's strategic priorities and flagship platforms.

Success Measures

Growth and quality of SFC's innovation pipeline, programmes, and ecosystem partnerships.
Delivery of strategic and hard KPIs, including outcomes linked to Enterprise Singapore-supported initiatives.
Strength of cross-pillar collaboration and the creation of long-term capabilities in the fashion venture and sustainability space.
Leadership effectiveness in building team capability, stakeholder confidence, and SFC's external profile in innovation and technology.

Required Skills and Competencies

Proven experience in innovation, venture building, ecosystem development, or strategic programme leadership, ideally within fashion, consumer, sustainability, technology, or adjacent sectors.
Strong understanding of the fashion and apparel landscape, with a clear interest in how technology and sustainability are reshaping the industry.
Ability to operate strategically and commercially, with experience setting direction, managing KPIs, and translating vision into executable programmes.
Strong stakeholder management skills, with the credibility to engage founders, industry leaders, corporate partners, government agencies, and investors.
Demonstrated people leadership, with the ability to mentor managers and develop team capability.
Entrepreneurial mindset, sound judgment, and comfort working in an evolving environment where programmes and partnerships are built iteratively.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to develop proposals, represent SFC publicly, and influence across stakeholders.
Strong project and budget management capabilities; familiarity with grant-linked deliverables and performance reporting is an advantage.
